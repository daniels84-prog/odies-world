Odie's World privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Odie's World is a free sensory play game for young children, made by a parent for his son. It is built to be safe for kids: it does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not ask for or read names, contacts, photos, location, or any other personal information, and it does not use the camera or microphone.

What stays on the device

The app keeps a copy of the newest version of the game on the device so it can be played without internet. This stays on the device and is never sent anywhere.

Internet use

When the device is online, the app checks daniels84-prog.github.io/odies-world for a newer version of the game and downloads it. These are plain file downloads with no information about the child or the device attached. The site is hosted by GitHub, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement. The app does not send any data of its own.

Permissions

The app uses internet access (for the update check above) and vibration (for little buzzes during play). It asks for nothing else.

Children

Odie's World is designed for children and complies with Google Play's Families policy. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for parents to review, correct, or delete.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.
