Full description (4000 characters max):
Odie's World is a sensory play game made by a dad for his 6-year-old son. Every corner is full of things to tap, drag, build and discover, with satisfying sounds and gentle vibrations. There's no score, no timer, and no way to lose. Just play.

WORKSHOP
Swing a hammer, cut wood with a saw, drill holes, weld shiny beads, paint, heat metal in a forge, throw sparks with an angle grinder, and run a giant power hammer.

POND
Open the tackle boxes and toss over 150 colorful lures into the water, watch the fish chase them, listen to the frogs croak, and guide a hungry rubber worm that grows longer with every bug it eats.

YARD
Catch glowing lightning bugs at night, collect eggs from the chickens, pan for rocks, blow leaves into a crackling campfire, mow the lawn, and dig tunnels underground with worms and colorful moles hunting for buried treasure.

SCIENCE
Crank a generator to light a bulb, and spray a water wheel to power a whole city.
